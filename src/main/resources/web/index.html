    How PlanPal works

    Trips

    Create a trip with a location, dates, a currency and a budget. Everything else hangs off it.

    Attendees

    Add the people coming. Only attendees can be part of a shared expense.

    Activities

    Give each plan a date, time and place. PlanPal checks it fits inside the trip and does not clash with anything already booked, then builds an itinerary with travel estimates between locations.

    Expenses

    Record what was paid, by whom, and in which currency. Split it evenly or pick exactly who shares it. Settle an expense once someone has paid it back.

    Who owes what

    PlanPal nets every expense out and shows each person a single number in your preferred display currency.

    Your profile

    Change your photo, bio, display name, username, home address and password. Follow other users to find them faster when adding attendees.
